About FloodGuard

Real-time flood monitoring and early warning for communities worldwide

Our Mission

To save lives and reduce property damage by providing timely, accurate flood warnings and empowering communities to take proactive measures.

Key Features

🤖

AI-Powered Prediction

Real-time flood risk prediction using machine learning models

📡

Multi-Channel Alerts

SMS, email, and in-app notifications for instant awareness

📍

Citizen Reporting

Photo and location-tagged reports from the community

🗺️

Interactive Maps

Live flood zone visualization with risk heatmaps

🛡️

Role-Based Dashboards

Tailored views for citizens, authorities, and administrators

🔗

Geographic Clustering

Incident clustering for better situational awareness

Technology Stack

FloodGuard is built with modern web technologies including Django (Python), PostGIS for spatial data, Leaflet for mapping, and incorporates machine learning models for flood risk prediction. The platform uses Celery for async task processing, Django Channels for real-time WebSocket updates, and Redis for caching and message brokering.
